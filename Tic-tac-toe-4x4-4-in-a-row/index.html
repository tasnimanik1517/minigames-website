Game Guide

Tic Tac Toe 4x4 – 4 in a Row

A bigger board, a sharper twist: each player only ever holds 4 pieces at a time, so the board is always shifting under your feet. Line up 4 in a row before your own pieces vanish on you.

How to Play

Only 4 pieces each. Place a 5th and your oldest piece vanishes. Get 4 in a row to win — before your own pieces disappear on you.

Tic Tac Toe 4x4 – 4 in a Row plays on a bigger 4-by-4 board with one big twist: each player only ever has 4 pieces in play at once. Tap any empty square to place a piece. Once you already have 4 on the board, placing a new one instantly removes your oldest piece, so the board never fully fills up and keeps changing shape. Get four of your marks in a straight line — any row, column, or diagonal — to win. Who moves first alternates each new round, so you'll open some games and respond in others, and the computer opponent replies instantly.

Strategy Tips

Because pieces disappear, a solid 4 in a row tic tac toe strategy here looks different from the classic game. Favor the eight squares that sit on a diagonal — they each belong to three different winning lines instead of two, so they create more chances at once. Before committing to a three-in-a-row setup, check which of your pieces is oldest: if it's part of that line, placing a fourth piece elsewhere will erase it before you can finish the line. Keep an eye on your opponent's aging pieces too — a line that looks blocked can open up the moment their oldest mark vanishes.

If you enjoy the fast, no-download logic games you'd find on sites like Coolmath Games, this version scratches the same itch with more depth. It keeps the instant, one-tap simplicity of browser tic-tac-toe, but the vanishing-piece twist means memorizing a few opening moves won't get you far — every game plays out differently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to play 4 by 4 tic tac toe?

Play on a 4×4 grid and take turns placing pieces with your opponent. In this version, each player is limited to 4 pieces on the board at once — placing a 5th piece automatically removes your own oldest one. Get 4 of your pieces in a row, column, or diagonal to win. Tap any empty square to play; the computer opponent responds instantly.

Is tic tac toe 3 or 4 in a row?

It depends on the board. Classic tic-tac-toe is played on a 3×3 grid and won with 3 in a row. Larger boards, like 4×4 or bigger, typically raise the target to 4 in a row, since a 3-in-a-row win would be too easy to reach with the extra space. This game uses a 4×4 board with a 4-in-a-row win condition.

Is 4x4 tic tac toe possible?

Yes — it's a real, playable variant, and it's been studied by mathematicians too. In the classic ruleset (no pieces removed), 4×4 tic-tac-toe with a 4-in-a-row win is known to end in a draw with perfect play, similar to standard tic-tac-toe. This game changes that by limiting each player to 4 pieces and making the oldest one vanish, which keeps the board constantly shifting and makes draws far less automatic.
