MiniGames.website is a free collection of small games you can play straight away in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.

What we make

Every game on the site is built for the site. We keep each one small so it loads in about a second, even on a slow mobile connection, and we test it with both touch and keyboard controls.

We start with games everyone already knows, like Snake, Tic Tac Toe and Rock Paper Scissors, and give them the extras that make them worth coming back to: difficulty levels, two-player modes, saved best scores and new twists on the rules.

How we keep it simple

  • No downloads and no accounts. Open a game and play.
  • No pop-ups and no redirects.
  • Games pause when you switch tabs, and the arrow keys control the game instead of scrolling the page.
  • Your scores and settings stay on your own device.

Free to play

The site is free. In the future it may show a small number of clearly labelled ads, placed away from the game so they never get in the way of play.
