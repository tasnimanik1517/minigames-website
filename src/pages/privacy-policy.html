This policy explains what information is processed when you use MiniGames.website (the "site") and the choices you have. In short: you don't need an account, your game data stays on your device, and we don't sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Games save things like your best score, difficulty level and sound setting in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure how fast pages load. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It processes technical information such as the page visited, the referring page, your browser type and your country.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses and request information, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising

To keep the games free, the site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Sharing buttons and links

Share buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to social networks unless you choose to share. When you follow a link to another website, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to certain processing. Because we don't run accounts, we hold very little information about you. To make a request, email hello@minigames.website.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change, and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@minigames.website.
