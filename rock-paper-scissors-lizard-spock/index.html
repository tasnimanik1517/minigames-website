Game Guide

Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock

An expanded five-move version of the classic hand game created by Sam Kass and Karen Bryla, and made famous by The Big Bang Theory. Designed for fast, tactical play with fewer draws.

How to Play

Choose one of five moves. Your opponent chooses one too. Each move beats two others and loses to two others. Whoever's move wins, wins the round.

Playing is instant. Simply tap any of the five buttons above (Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, or Spock). The computer chooses at the same time, and the winner is announced immediately along with your live score.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the rules to rock paper scissors lizard spock?

Each of the five moves beats two others and loses to the other two. Rock beats Scissors and Lizard. Paper beats Rock and Spock. Scissors beats Paper and Lizard. Lizard beats Spock and Paper. Spock beats Scissors and Rock. Both players pick a move at the same time — whichever move wins, that player wins the round. The same move from both sides means a draw.

What is Sheldon's version of rock-paper-scissors?

Sheldon Cooper, a character from The Big Bang Theory, suggests this five-move version instead of regular Rock Paper Scissors, arguing that people who know each other well tend to draw too often in the basic three-move game. The show presents it as his own idea, but the game had already been created earlier by Sam Kass and Karen Bryla.

Why does paper disprove Spock?

It's simply a playful line from the show — there's no real logic behind it. Each winning pairing in the five-move game was given a small, fun reason (for example, Scissors cuts Paper), and Paper beating Spock was given the fun label "paper disproves Spock." It's just flavor text, not a scientific explanation.

What is the meaning of "Rock-paper-scissors-lizard-Spock"?

It's an expanded version of classic Rock Paper Scissors, with two extra moves — Lizard and Spock — added to the original three. With five moves instead of three, the chance of a draw drops (from 1 in 3 to 1 in 5), which makes the game feel less repetitive and more unpredictable.

Sam Kass, together with Karen Bryla, created this five-move game, mainly to cut down on how often games end in a draw between people who know each other's habits well. It later became famous worldwide after being featured on The Big Bang Theory through Sheldon's character.

Rock paper scissors lizard spock hand signs

Rock is a closed fist. Paper is a flat, open hand. Scissors is two fingers spread in a V shape. Lizard is fingers pinched together like a duck's beak. Spock is the Vulcan salute from Star Trek — the hand split into two pairs of fingers.
