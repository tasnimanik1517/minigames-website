About

MiniGames.website is a small, growing collection of free browser games you can play instantly — no downloads, no sign-ups, no accounts. Every game runs directly in your browser and works on both desktop and mobile.

Why this site exists

It's built and maintained by a solo creator who enjoys making fast, simple, no-nonsense games — both original ones and fun variations on classic favorites.
